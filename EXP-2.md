# AIM:
To observe and measure the frequency deviation and the modulation index of FM.

# APPARATUS REQUIRED:
Modulus ACL-03 Power supply Oscilloscope Voltmeter Frequency meter Connecting wires Function generator

# THEORY:
Frequency modulation is a type of modulation in which the frequency of the high frequency (carrier) is varied in accordance with the instantaneous value of the modulating signal.
f and MODULATION INDEX m f :
f represents the maximum shift between the modulated signal frequency, over and under the frequency of the carrier.
We define modulation index m f the ratio between	f and the modulating frequency

# FREQUENCY MODULATION GENERATION:
The circuits used to generate a frequency modulation must vary the frequency of a high frequency signal (carrier) as function of the amplitude of a low frequency signal (modulating signal). In practice there are two main methods used to generate FM.

# PROCEDURE:

Connect the power supply with the proper polarity to ACL-03 and while connecting ensure that the power supply is OFF.
Connect the output of FG to the modulation input with the proper frequency. Switch ON the power supply.
